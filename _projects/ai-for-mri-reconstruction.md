My PhD project, **Reliable Cortical Interface Diffusion Imaging via Physics-Informed Deep Learning**, develops physics-informed implicit neural representations for diffusion MRI from k-space data.

The goal is to enable geometrically accurate, high-resolution in vivo diffusion imaging across cortical layers within feasible scan time. This would make it possible to study subtle cortical abnormalities, including changes related to diseases such as multiple sclerosis, at a spatial scale that is difficult to access with conventional diffusion MRI.

This work builds on my MSc thesis on deep learning-based correction of susceptibility and eddy-current distortions in ultra-high-field diffusion MRI.
