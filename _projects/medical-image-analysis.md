For my MSc thesis at DTU and DRCMR, I worked on deep learning for correcting susceptibility and eddy-current distortions in ultra-high-field diffusion MRI.

The project addressed a central challenge in 7T brain diffusion imaging: severe geometric distortions caused by susceptibility effects, eddy currents, and head motion. I developed a physics-informed deep learning framework for deformable image registration that uses structural MRI as an anatomical reference and does not rely on reverse phase-encoding acquisitions.

The work motivates my PhD direction: moving from image-space correction toward k-space-domain modeling for reliable high-resolution cortical diffusion imaging.
